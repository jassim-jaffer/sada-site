What you can do with it
Self guided tours

Go at your
own pace.

The audio begins as you reach each stop. Your phone stays in your pocket, your eyes stay on the place.

Point maps

Wander.
Listen where you stand.

Maps built by your favourite creators, each around a theme — a scatter of pins with no set order. Open one, follow whatever catches you, and hear what happened right there.

Pins & audio

A pin is a place
with something to hear.

Every pin on the map marks a spot where a voice was left — a story, a memory, a walk that starts here. Tap it and it plays exactly where it happened — no ceremony, no staring at a screen.

The closer you stand, the more the place has to say.

Real voices,
in real places.

Every tour and map on sāda is told by people — guides, historians, fishermen, neighbours.

And you can leave a voice of your own at a place — a memory, a moment, a story told where it happened. We call that an echo. It's what sāda means — صدى, the echo — and every one left keeps a place speaking for the next person who stands there.
