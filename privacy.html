Privacy Policy

Sada — audio exploration. Last updated 23 September 2026.

Sada is made by Hakawati LLC, Muscat, Oman. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We have tried to write it in plain language rather than legal padding.

We do not sell your data. We do not show advertising. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Name and email addressTo create your account and sign you in.
Location, including in the backgroundTo show what is near you, to pin an echo to the place you left it, and — during a guided walk — to start audio automatically as you reach a stop. Background location is used only while a walk is active.
Audio you recordThese are echoes. They are the content of the app and are published to the place you left them.
Photos you addOnly photos you choose to attach to an echo.
Usage and diagnosticsCrash reports and basic usage, so we can find faults and fix them.
Answers at the end of a tourWhen you finish a tour, we may ask a few optional one-tap questions — for example where you are from, or whether you would stop for a snack or a drink nearby. Your answers are not linked to your name or account. We keep them with a random ID for your device, how much of the tour you heard, and whether you were at its last stop (a yes or no, not your location). We use them to improve tours, and we share them only as totals, never individually — including with the places and businesses we work with.
Purchase statusIf you buy anything, we record that you bought it. Payment card details go to Apple or Google and never reach us.

Your location, specifically

Sada asks for location permission because the app is built around where you are standing. You choose what to grant:

  • While using the app — enough to explore the map and leave echoes.
  • Always — needed only if you want audio on a guided walk to play by itself while your phone is in your pocket. You can decline this and still use Sada.

We store the location attached to an echo, because that is the point of an echo. We do not build a history of your movements, and we do not sell location data to anyone.

What other people can see

An echo you publish is public: other people can hear it, see where it was left, and see the name on your profile. Do not record anything you would not want a stranger standing in that spot to hear.

Reporting and blocking

Sada carries recordings made by other people. If something is offensive, in the wrong place, or should not be there, use Report on the echo. Reports are reviewed and content that breaks our rules is removed. You can also block someone so their echoes no longer reach you.

Your rights

  • See your data — ask us and we will tell you what we hold.
  • Delete an echo — you can delete anything you have published, at any time, in the app.
  • Delete your account — available in the app, under your profile. This removes your account, your profile and your personal details. The echoes you published are taken down at the same time: they stop appearing on the map, in the feed and anywhere else in Sada. The recordings themselves are kept in our storage, no longer linked to a public profile, so that a deletion made by mistake can be undone if you ask us. If you would like them erased permanently as well, tell us when you delete (or email us) and we will remove them. Some records may persist briefly in backups before being overwritten.
  • Correct your details — edit them in your profile, or ask us.

To make any request, email jassim@dauboman.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

How long we keep things

Your account and echoes are kept until you delete them or close your account. When you close your account, your profile and personal details are removed and your echoes are taken down from Sada; the recordings stay in our storage, unlinked from any public profile, unless you ask us to erase them. Crash and diagnostic data is kept for up to 12 months.

Children

Sada is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Where your data lives

Sada is operated from Oman. Our service providers may store and process data on servers outside Oman. We choose providers that apply recognised security standards.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
