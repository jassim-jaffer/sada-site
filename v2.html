What is Sada?

An audio-first storytelling app. Listen to voices linked to the exact place where you stand. Stories you won't find in guide books.

Two formats to choose from. Take a walking tour and learn about your surroundings. Or go on a quest and follow clues to get a prize

One listening point leads to another. Before you know it you've explored where you are and learned something – many things! - new along the way.

Voiced by real people. Historians, artists, wellbeing practitioners and people whose roots go back many generations in this exact place.

How it works

1

Download the app and switch on your location.

2

Explore what's near you, and make your way to the starting point. The voice plays automatically.

3

Follow the voice, explore the place, learn something new along the way.

Why Sada

Sada means echo. It is the Arabic word for the sound a place gives back to you — صدى.

Every tour, walk and quest is told by someone with a real connection to where you are standing: historians, artists, wellbeing practitioners, and people whose families have been there for generations. You hear them in the place their story belongs to, not on a screen somewhere else.

Meet the Founders

Say hello to Jasim Al Lawati and Roshni Goyate, Sada's co-founders, and husband-wife duo. Together they bring over 40 years' experience in storytelling, through TV, film, tech, marketing, poetry and more.
